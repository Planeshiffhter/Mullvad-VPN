SELECTED VPN REPOSITORIES
=========================
Project: https://github.com/rejoansardar23/vpn
Decision recorded: 2026-11-15

These are the EXACT TWO earlier core NETWORKING-COMPONENT choices for our VPN
platform; they are not the winners of the later independent COUNTRY-ARCHITECTURE
research (2026-11-15). The exact TWO architecture-STUDY selections are
mullvad/mullvadvpn-app and amnezia-vpn/amnezia-client (GPL-3.0; do not copy).
Read docs/PROJECT_MEMORY.md and
docs/knowledge-archive/REPOSITORY_RESEARCH.md to keep these distinct.

1. apify/proxy-chain
   GitHub: https://github.com/apify/proxy-chain
   Purpose: Browser proxy gateway and HTTP/HTTPS CONNECT forwarding.
   License: Apache-2.0.
   Current decision: KEEP the existing integration.
   Current project dependency: proxy-chain@3.0.3 in the feat/tested-private-browser-proxy branch.
   Do not replace working integration without documented technical evidence.

2. WireGuard/wireguard-go
   GitHub mirror: https://github.com/WireGuard/wireguard-go
   Official upstream: https://git.zx2c4.com/wireguard-go
   Purpose: Future full-device WireGuard VPN tunneling.
   License: MIT.
   Current decision: SELECT FOR FUTURE NATIVE VPN PHASE; NOT YET INTEGRATED.
   Audit and pin an exact upstream revision before integration.
   Use platform-native/kernel WireGuard alternatives where technically preferable.
